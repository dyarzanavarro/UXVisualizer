The gap

Per-page audits are a solved problem. That's the issue.

LIFT, Baymard, Cialdini — checked against any single page, for free, by a prompt kit. The differentiator isn't scoring pages harder. It's scoring the connections between them, where fragmentation actually happens.

What everyone already checks

  • Landing page heuristics, in isolation
  • Checkout friction, in isolation
  • Trust badges present or absent, per page
  • Generic "add urgency" recommendations

What gets missed every time

  • Does the price on step 1 match step 3?
  • Does trust build toward checkout, or reset?
  • Is every headline promise actually fulfilled downstream?
  • Does tone survive the handoff between teams/tools?
